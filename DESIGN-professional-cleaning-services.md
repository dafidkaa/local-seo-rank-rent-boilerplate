# DESIGN BRIEF — Professional Cleaning Services

> **Wired in code:** `themePreset("cleaning")` -> fresh blue #0369a1 / cyan #06b6d4 | Plus Jakarta Sans + DM Sans | round corners, gradient CTAs, solid nav.
> Read **DESIGN-SYSTEM.md** first — it carries the global rules (type, nav variants,
> buttons, motion, imagery, anti-slop checklist). This brief adds the niche layer.

## Positioning & feel
Crisp, light-flooded, systematized. The design itself should feel cleaned.

## Palette & accent discipline
Preset palette: fresh blue #0369a1 / cyan #06b6d4. Cyan for CTAs and sparkle moments; deep blue for structure. White space is the product demo.

## Typography
Preset pairing: **Plus Jakarta Sans + DM Sans** (weights wired automatically).

## Navigation & hero
Static hero + embedded quote form — gleaming interior photo, fresh-air palette. Solid nav.
- Primary CTA: **"Get a Cleaning Quote"** | Secondary: **"Book Recurring Service"**
- Form strategy: **standard — embedded hero/contact forms plus popup CTAs on inner pages**

## Section rhythm
Follow the standard service/location templates (hero -> quick answer -> stats ->
sub-services -> signs -> pros/cons -> comparison -> why-us -> process -> gallery ->
area -> FAQ -> CTA) with the cadence rules from DESIGN-SYSTEM.md s6. Emphasize the
signature sections below; cut anything that would be filler for this niche.

## Signature sections (build these well)
1. Whats-included checklist per package — the conversion core
2. Before/after slider on deep-clean pages
3. Recurring plans ComparisonTable (one-time / weekly / fortnightly)
4. Products & method panel — eco lines only if genuinely used

## Imagery direction
Sun-flooded clean interiors, microfiber and suds macro, gloved hand on chrome, neatly arranged supplies.

**Unsplash starting queries** (verify every result actually shows the subject):
- "bright clean living room sunlight"
- "microfiber cloth cleaning closeup"
- "cleaning supplies neatly arranged"
- "sparkling kitchen chrome faucet"
- "professional cleaner apartment"
- "fresh towels folded spa"

**Never:** hazmat-looking suits, filthy shock photos, mop-bucket stock from 2005.

## Copy tone
Checklist clarity. Rooms, tasks, frequencies — concrete nouns.

## Do
- Lightest visual weight of all trades — tints over darks
- Round corners; the brand is soft and fresh
- Show the actual checklist; vagueness kills cleaning quotes

## Don't
- Sparkle-GIF energy — one subtle shine max
- More than one dark section
- Stock germs/bacteria scare visuals

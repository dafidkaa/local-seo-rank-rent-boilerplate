# Niche Design Brief: Professional Cleaning Services
This document defines the visual guidelines, typography, color palette, and asset map for the **Professional Cleaning Services** niche, encompassing residential, commercial, and specialized cleaning operations.

## 1. Brand Identity & Visual Strategy
- **Tone & Mood:** The brand identity for professional cleaning services must exude **trustworthiness, reliability, and pristine cleanliness**. The overall mood should be **calm, efficient, and reassuring**, instilling confidence in clients that their spaces will be handled with utmost care and professionalism. For residential services, an additional layer of **friendliness and approachability** is crucial, emphasizing a stress-free experience. Commercial services, conversely, should lean into **corporate sophistication, efficiency, and meticulous attention to detail**. Eco-friendly or specialized cleaning services can further integrate themes of **health, sustainability, and advanced expertise**. Visuals should convey a sense of order, freshness, and sparkling results, utilizing bright, clean imagery and a clear, uncluttered aesthetic to reinforce the promise of a spotless environment.

- **Typography Pairings:**
  - **Headings:** **Montserrat** (Google Font) - A modern, geometric sans-serif that conveys professionalism and clarity. Use weights 700 (Bold) for main titles and 600 (Semi-Bold) for subheadings. Letter-spacing: -0.02em.
  - **Body Text:** **Open Sans** (Google Font) - A highly readable and versatile sans-serif, offering a friendly yet professional feel. Use weights 400 (Regular) and 300 (Light) for general content. Letter-spacing: 0.01em.

## 4. High-Converting Component Layouts
To ensure the website not only looks professional but also drives conversions, the following CSS/Tailwind design specifications must be applied to key components.

### A. The Niche-Specific Hero Section
- **Layout:** Split layout (`md:flex md:flex-row`) or a full-width background image with a centered content card. For cleaning services, a split layout often works best: text and CTA on the left (`md:w-1/2`), and a high-quality, bright image on the right (`md:w-1/2`).
- **Background:** If using a full-width image, apply a subtle gradient overlay to ensure text readability: `bg-gradient-to-r from-white/90 via-white/70 to-transparent` (adjust colors based on the selected palette).
- **Typography:**
  - H1: `text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-bold text-gray-900 leading-tight mb-4`
  - Subheadline: `text-lg md:text-xl text-gray-700 mb-8 max-w-2xl`
- **CTA Buttons:**
  - Primary: `bg-primary text-white font-semibold py-3 px-8 rounded-lg shadow-lg hover:bg-primary-dark hover:shadow-xl transition-all duration-300 transform hover:-translate-y-1`
  - Secondary (e.g., "Call Now"): `bg-transparent border-2 border-primary text-primary font-semibold py-3 px-8 rounded-lg hover:bg-primary/10 transition-all duration-300`
- **Trust Badges:** Place small, recognizable icons (e.g., "Fully Insured", "5-Star Rated") immediately below the CTA buttons: `flex items-center space-x-4 mt-6 text-sm text-gray-600`.

### B. Service/Product Grid Cards
- **Layout:** CSS Grid for responsive display: `grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3 gap-8`.
- **Card Container:** `bg-white rounded-xl overflow-hidden shadow-md hover:shadow-2xl transition-shadow duration-300 border border-gray-100 group`.
- **Image Container:** `relative h-48 overflow-hidden`. The image inside should scale slightly on hover: `w-full h-full object-cover transform group-hover:scale-105 transition-transform duration-500`.
- **Content Area:** `p-6`.
  - Icon/Badge: A small, colored icon representing the service (e.g., a sparkle icon for deep cleaning) placed at the top of the content area: `text-accent w-8 h-8 mb-4`.
  - Title: `text-xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-2`.
  - Description: `text-gray-600 text-sm mb-4 line-clamp-3`.
- **Action Link:** A subtle "Learn More" or "Get a Quote" link that reveals an arrow on hover: `text-primary font-semibold flex items-center group-hover:text-primary-dark`.

### C. Trust Signals & Interactive Elements
- **Testimonial Slider:**
  - Container: `bg-gray-50 py-16`.
  - Card: `bg-white p-8 rounded-2xl shadow-sm border border-gray-100 relative`.
  - Quote Icon: A large, faint quote mark in the background: `absolute top-4 right-4 text-gray-100 text-6xl font-serif`.
  - Rating: 5 gold stars (`text-yellow-400`) displayed prominently above the text.
- **Interactive Estimate Form:**
  - Container: `bg-white p-8 rounded-2xl shadow-xl border-t-4 border-primary`.
  - Input Fields: `w-full px-4 py-3 rounded-lg border border-gray-300 focus:ring-2 focus:ring-primary focus:border-primary transition-colors bg-gray-50 focus:bg-white`.
  - Select Dropdowns: Use custom styling for service selection (e.g., "House Cleaning", "Office Cleaning") to make it look premium.
  - Submit Button: Full width, highly visible: `w-full bg-accent text-white font-bold py-4 rounded-lg shadow-md hover:bg-accent-dark transition-colors text-lg`.

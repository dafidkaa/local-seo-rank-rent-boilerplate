# DESIGN BRIEF — Childcare & Early Education

> **Wired in code:** `themePreset("education")` -> indigo #4338ca / warm yellow #f59e0b | Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter | round corners, gradient CTAs, solid nav.
> Read **DESIGN-SYSTEM.md** first — it carries the global rules (type, nav variants,
> buttons, motion, imagery, anti-slop checklist). This brief adds the niche layer.

## Positioning & feel
Sunlit, safe, gently playful. Premium here means calm colors and visible care, not cartoon chaos.

## Palette & accent discipline
Preset palette: indigo #4338ca / warm yellow #f59e0b. Yellow as sunshine moments — CTAs and small shapes. Indigo softened with extra tints; round everything.

## Typography
Preset pairing: **Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter** (weights wired automatically).
Optional premium upgrade: **Soft Rounded — Varela Round + Nunito Sans** — set `fontDisplay`/`fontBody` AND
`fontsHref: "https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Varela+Round&family=Nunito+Sans:wght@400;600;700;800&display=swap"` (this family set needs an explicit URL).

## Navigation & hero
Static hero — bright playroom or craft close-up, warm H1, Schedule-a-Visit CTA. Solid nav.
- Primary CTA: **"Schedule a Visit"** | Secondary: **"Ask About Enrollment"**
- Form strategy: **standard — embedded hero/contact forms plus popup CTAs on inner pages**

## Section rhythm
Follow the standard service/location templates (hero -> quick answer -> stats ->
sub-services -> signs -> pros/cons -> comparison -> why-us -> process -> gallery ->
area -> FAQ -> CTA) with the cadence rules from DESIGN-SYSTEM.md s6. Emphasize the
signature sections below; cut anything that would be filler for this niche.

## Signature sections (build these well)
1. Day-in-the-life timeline — morning to pickup, with photos per block
2. Safety & staff panel — ratios, certifications, security (real, specific)
3. Rooms/ages grid — per-age-group cards with capacity chips
4. Enrollment steps + waitlist honesty

## Imagery direction
Bright playrooms, craft-table close-ups (hands, paint, blocks), outdoor play equipment, reading corners. Children's faces minimal/oblique — privacy first.

**Unsplash starting queries** (verify every result actually shows the subject):
- "kindergarten playroom bright"
- "children painting hands closeup"
- "wooden toys montessori shelf"
- "outdoor playground sunny"
- "reading corner kids books"
- "teacher helping child craft"

**Never:** identifiable children's faces (privacy), clip-art rainbows, chaotic mess.

## Copy tone
Parent-to-parent warmth with administrator precision (hours, ratios, menus).

## Do
- Soft Rounded font pairing fits if the brand skews younger
- Generous tints of accent-light for section backgrounds
- Show real daily schedules and food menus

## Don't
- Comic fonts and primary-color riots
- Stock kids pointing at camera
- Vague safety claims — specifics or silence

## Why Asking The Right Questions Matters

Before you book any local service, taking five minutes to ask the right questions can save you time, money, and frustration. This checklist covers the essentials.

### Questions About Pricing

1. **Can you provide an itemized estimate?** A clear breakdown helps you understand what you're paying for and makes it easier to compare providers.

2. **Are there any additional fees I should expect?** Some providers charge extra for materials, travel, or weekend scheduling. Get this in writing.

3. **What payment methods do you accept?** Know upfront whether they take cards, checks, or digital payments, and when payment is due.

### Questions About Scheduling

- **When can you start?** Understanding lead times helps you plan.
- **How long will the job take?** Get a realistic timeframe, not just a best-case scenario.
- **What happens if weather or delays affect the schedule?** Know their policy for rescheduling.

### Questions About Scope And Quality

> "The single most important question: What happens if something goes wrong?"

Ask about:

- **Guarantees or warranties** on the work performed
- **Who performs the actual work** — the person you're speaking with or a subcontracted crew
- **Clean-up and site condition** after the job is done
- **Permits or approvals** needed and who handles them

### Questions About Experience

| Question | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| How long have you been in business? | Stability and track record |
| Have you done similar jobs in my area? | Local experience counts |
| Can I see examples or references? | Verifiable proof of quality |
| Are your workers trained and insured? | Safety and liability |

## After The Job

Don't forget to ask about follow-up:

- Will you receive a written summary of what was done?
- Is there a maintenance recommendation or next step?
- What's the process if you notice an issue after they leave?

## Quick Pre-Booking Checklist

Before you say yes:

- [ ] You have a written estimate
- [ ] The scope is clearly defined
- [ ] You understand the timeline
- [ ] Insurance and licensing are confirmed
- [ ] Payment terms are agreed upon
- [ ] You know the follow-up process

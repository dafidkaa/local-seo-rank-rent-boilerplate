# DESIGN BRIEF — Local Education & Tutoring

> **Wired in code:** `themePreset("education")` -> indigo #4338ca / warm yellow #f59e0b | Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter | round corners, gradient CTAs, solid nav.
> Read **DESIGN-SYSTEM.md** first — it carries the global rules (type, nav variants,
> buttons, motion, imagery, anti-slop checklist). This brief adds the niche layer.

## Positioning & feel
Optimistic rigor. Indigo seriousness with a warm yellow aha — premium but encouraging.

## Palette & accent discipline
Preset palette: indigo #4338ca / warm yellow #f59e0b. Yellow for CTAs, progress numerals, and highlight marks (like a marker pen). Indigo structures everything.

## Typography
Preset pairing: **Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter** (weights wired automatically).

## Navigation & hero
Static hero + embedded assessment form — the parent-buyer wants action. Solid nav.
- Primary CTA: **"Book a Free Assessment"** | Secondary: **"Meet Our Tutors"**
- Form strategy: **standard — embedded hero/contact forms plus popup CTAs on inner pages**

## Section rhythm
Follow the standard service/location templates (hero -> quick answer -> stats ->
sub-services -> signs -> pros/cons -> comparison -> why-us -> process -> gallery ->
area -> FAQ -> CTA) with the cadence rules from DESIGN-SYSTEM.md s6. Emphasize the
signature sections below; cut anything that would be filler for this niche.

## Signature sections (build these well)
1. Results stats band — grade improvements, pass rates (real cohorts only)
2. Method steps — assessment, plan, sessions, review (ProcessSection)
3. Subject grid with level chips (primary / exam-prep / advanced)
4. Tutor profiles — qualifications, subjects, one personable line

## Imagery direction
Focused study moments, notebooks and annotation close-ups, bright tidy desks, one-on-one tutoring across a table.

**Unsplash starting queries** (verify every result actually shows the subject):
- "student studying focused desk"
- "tutor explaining one on one"
- "notebook math notes closeup"
- "bright study room minimal"
- "teenager laptop learning home"
- "stack of books warm light"

**Never:** staged classroom cheering, apple-on-desk cliches, stressed-kid stock.

## Copy tone
Speaks to parents, respects the student. Concrete outcomes over promises.

## Do
- Marker-style yellow underline accents on key phrases (CSS, sparingly)
- Round corners + friendly chips; serious does not mean stiff
- FAQ covering pricing, scheduling, progress reporting

## Don't
- Guaranteeing grades
- Childish illustration packs on a tutoring brand
- Walls of accreditation logos without context

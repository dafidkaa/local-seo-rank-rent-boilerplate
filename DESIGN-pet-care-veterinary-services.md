# DESIGN BRIEF — Pet Care & Veterinary

> **Wired in code:** `themePreset("pet-care")` -> teal #0d9488 / coral #f97316 | Nunito + Inter | round corners, gradient CTAs, solid nav.
> Read **DESIGN-SYSTEM.md** first — it carries the global rules (type, nav variants,
> buttons, motion, imagery, anti-slop checklist). This brief adds the niche layer.

## Positioning & feel
Warm competence. Playful at the edges, clinical where it counts.

## Palette & accent discipline
Preset palette: teal #0d9488 / coral #f97316. Coral for CTAs and playful chips; teal holds the medical trust.

## Typography
Preset pairing: **Nunito + Inter** (weights wired automatically).

## Navigation & hero
Static hero — one great animal portrait + warm H1. Solid nav. (Pets are the one hero where a face always wins.)
- Primary CTA: **"Book a Visit"** | Secondary: **"Meet the Team"**
- Form strategy: **standard — embedded hero/contact forms plus popup CTAs on inner pages**

## Section rhythm
Follow the standard service/location templates (hero -> quick answer -> stats ->
sub-services -> signs -> pros/cons -> comparison -> why-us -> process -> gallery ->
area -> FAQ -> CTA) with the cadence rules from DESIGN-SYSTEM.md s6. Emphasize the
signature sections below; cut anything that would be filler for this niche.

## Signature sections (build these well)
1. Services by pet type — tab or chip filter (dogs/cats/exotics)
2. Team cards WITH animals — the one niche where staff photos convert
3. Facility tour gallery — exam rooms, grooming stations, boarding suites
4. Wellness plan ComparisonTable — puppy/adult/senior tiers

## Imagery direction
Candid pets (head tilts, paws macro), gentle exam moments, grooming results, play areas.

**Unsplash starting queries** (verify every result actually shows the subject):
- "dog head tilt portrait studio"
- "veterinarian examining cat gentle"
- "puppy paws closeup"
- "dog grooming result fluffy"
- "cat relaxing clinic"
- "pets playing daycare"

**Never:** cone-of-shame sad pets, surgical scenes, stocky white-coat poses.

## Copy tone
Talks to the human, winks at the pet. Medical facts in plain words.

## Do
- Round radius + coral chips = the friendly layer
- Emergency info findable in 2 seconds (header + footer)
- Real team-and-their-pets photos beat any stock

## Don't
- Paw-print wallpaper patterns
- Mixing grooming-cute and surgery-serious in one section
- Hiding euthanasia/serious-care pages — handle with funeral-grade gentleness

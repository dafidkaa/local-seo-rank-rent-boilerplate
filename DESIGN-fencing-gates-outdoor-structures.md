## Signature sections (build these well)
1. Material comparison table — wood vs metal vs composite (cost, lifespan, upkeep)
2. Gallery by material/style with lightbox — buyers browse by look
3. Property-line & permits explainer — the FAQ gold of this niche
4. Warranty/workmanship panel with plain terms

# DESIGN BRIEF — Funeral & Memorial Services

> **Wired in code:** `themePreset("funeral")` -> slate #334155 / soft gold #a16207 | EB Garamond + Inter | soft corners, solid CTAs, overlay nav.
> Read **DESIGN-SYSTEM.md** first — it carries the global rules (type, nav variants,
> buttons, motion, imagery, anti-slop checklist). This brief adds the niche layer.

## Positioning & feel
Dignity, calm, room to breathe. Design must never perform; it holds the door open.

## Palette & accent discipline
Preset palette: slate #334155 / soft gold #a16207. Soft gold for hairline rules and the single CTA. Slate everywhere else. Nothing saturated.

## Typography
Preset pairing: **EB Garamond + Inter** (weights wired automatically).

## Navigation & hero
Static hero, overlay nav — soft landscape or light-filled interior, serif H1, one gentle CTA. Slowest, quietest page in the catalog.
- Primary CTA: **"Speak With Us — 24/7"** | Secondary: **"Plan Ahead"**
- Form strategy: **premium — popup inquiry modal from CTAs; embedded form only on the contact page**

## Section rhythm
Follow the standard service/location templates (hero -> quick answer -> stats ->
sub-services -> signs -> pros/cons -> comparison -> why-us -> process -> gallery ->
area -> FAQ -> CTA) with the cadence rules from DESIGN-SYSTEM.md s6. Emphasize the
signature sections below; cut anything that would be filler for this niche.

## Signature sections (build these well)
1. Immediate-need vs pre-planning fork — two clear paths above the fold
2. What-to-expect steps written in gentle plain language (ProcessSection)
3. Facilities gallery — light interiors, flowers, gardens
4. Practical guidance hub links (documents, costs, timelines) — quiet authority

## Imagery direction
Soft window light, single flowers, candles, calm landscapes, peaceful rooms. Never caskets close-up, never grieving faces.

**Unsplash starting queries** (verify every result actually shows the subject):
- "white lily single soft light"
- "candle flame calm dark"
- "peaceful garden path morning"
- "chapel interior light window"
- "white roses closeup gentle"
- "calm lake sunrise mist"

**Never:** cemeteries-at-dusk drama, hands-clasped stock grief, dark gothic anything.

## Copy tone
Short sentences, second person, zero euphemism-stacking. Warmth without sentimentality.

## Do
- Motion nearly off: reveals only, no count-ups, no sliders
- Phone CTA prominent — calls dominate this niche
- Costs addressed plainly; opacity creates anxiety

## Don't
- Urgency mechanics of any kind
- Stock photos of actors crying
- Dark gradient sections — keep every band light

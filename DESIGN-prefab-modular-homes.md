# Niche Design Brief: Prefab & Modular Homes Construction

This document defines the visual guidelines, typography, color palette, and asset map for the **Prefabricated Houses, Modular Homes, Kit Houses, and Eco-Friendly Residential Construction** niche.

Croatian examples: *montažne kuće zadar, montažne kuće pula, montažne kuće varaždin*

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Visual Strategy

### Tone & Mood

The prefab and modular homes market sits at the intersection of **pragmatic efficiency and aspirational living**.
Buyers are typically cost-conscious families, young couples, and eco-aware individuals who want quality construction
without the chaos of traditional builds. The emotional driver is **control** — over budget, timeline, and outcome.

Visual communication must project **precision, cleanliness, and warmth simultaneously**.
Think architectural blueprints softened by natural wood textures and green landscaping.
The brand should feel like a trusted engineer who also understands what "home" means emotionally.

Trust signals are paramount. This audience has done research, compared quotes, and read horror stories online.
Every design choice — from font weight to button copy — must reinforce **reliability, transparency, and expertise**.
Certifications, process timelines, and real project photos outperform stock imagery by a wide margin.

### Typography Pairings

**Preset 1 — Modern Modular (Recommended Default)**

- **Display / Headings:** `Syne` — Weight 700, letter-spacing `-0.02em`
- **Body / Paragraphs:** `Inter` — Weight 400/500, letter-spacing `0.01em`
- **Accent Labels / Badges:** `Inter` — Weight 600 uppercase, letter-spacing `0.08em`

**Preset 2 — Eco Warmth**

- **Display / Headings:** `DM Serif Display` — Weight 400, letter-spacing `-0.01em`
- **Body / Paragraphs:** `Source Sans 3` — Weight 400, letter-spacing `0.01em`

**Preset 3 — Scandinavian Minimal**

- **Display / Headings:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` — Weight 800, letter-spacing `-0.03em`
- **Body / Paragraphs:** `Nunito Sans` — Weight 300/400, letter-spacing `0.02em`

> **Rule:** Never pair two serif fonts. Always maintain a minimum 3:1 contrast ratio between heading and body weights.

## 3. Section Architecture & Word Count Distribution

To hit the **1,000–1,500 word density target**, organize sections with alternating light/dark layouts:

---

### 1. Hero Slider — 150 words

**Layout:** Full-viewport slider with 2–3 slides, dark overlay, centered text block.

**Content Strategy:**
Lead with the primary value proposition on slide 1: speed-to-completion and cost certainty.
Slide 2 targets eco-conscious buyers with sustainability messaging.
Slide 3 shows a finished family home with social proof copy ("47 homes delivered in 2024").
Each slide has a **primary CTA** ("Get a Free Estimate") and a **secondary CTA** ("View Our Projects").
Include a floating trust badge strip at the bottom of the hero: ISO certification, years in business, homes built.

---

### 2. Trust Bar — 50 words

**Layout:** Full-width light-grey bar, 5 icon+stat columns, no background image.

**Content Strategy:**
Display five micro-trust signals in a single horizontal row:
years of experience, number of completed homes, average build time in weeks,
energy efficiency rating, and a customer satisfaction score.
Use bold numerals with small descriptive labels beneath each stat.

---

### 3. Welcome / Story Section — 250 words

**Layout:** Two-column split — left is editorial text, right is the `about` image with a floating accent card.

**Content Strategy:**
Open with a relatable pain point: traditional construction is unpredictable, expensive, and stressful.
Introduce the company as the modern alternative — factory precision meets on-site craftsmanship.
Weave in the local angle (e.g., "serving Zadar, Pula, and Varaždin since 2009").
The floating accent card on the image should display a key differentiator stat
(e.g., "Built in 8–12 weeks on average").
Close with a soft CTA linking to the About page or process section.

---

### 4. Service Grid — 200 words

**Layout:** 2×2 card grid on desktop, single column on mobile. Each card uses the service image keys.

**Content Strategy:**
Four core service cards:
**Custom Modular Homes**, **Eco Prefab Packages**, **Kit House Assembly**, **Commercial Modular Units**.
Each card gets a 30–40 word description focusing on the buyer benefit, not the technical process.
Use action-oriented headlines: "Move In Faster", "Build Greener", "Design Your Way".
Every card links to a dedicated service detail page for SEO depth.

---

### 5. Why Choose Us — 200 words

**Layout:** Dark background section (Primary color), 3-column icon grid with short paragraphs.

**Content Strategy:**
Three differentiators presented as icon + headline + 40-word paragraph:
**Price Transparency** (fixed quotes, no hidden costs),
**Speed of Delivery** (factory-built modules reduce on-site time by 60%),
**Certified Quality** (EN standards, energy class A+).
This section directly addresses the three biggest objections in the prefab market:
"Will it be cheap-looking?", "Will it run over budget?", "Is it really durable?"

---

### 6. Our Process — 200 words

**Layout:** Horizontal numbered timeline on desktop, vertical accordion on mobile.

**Content Strategy:**
Five-step process: **Consultation → Design & Permit → Factory Build → Site Delivery → Handover**.
Each step gets a 30-word description and an estimated timeframe.
Emphasize that steps 3 and 4 happen in parallel with permit processing —
this is a key competitive advantage that reduces total project time.
End the section with a reassuring line: "You'll always know exactly where your home is in the process."

---

### 7. Interactive Estimate Form — 150 words

**Layout:** Full-width section with a soft background texture, centered two-column form.

**Content Strategy:**
Multi-step form with 3 screens:
**Step 1** — Home size selector (dropdown: 50m², 80m², 100m², 120m², custom).
**Step 2** — Package selector (Basic / Standard / Premium / Eco+) with tooltip descriptions.
**Step 3** — Contact details + preferred contact method + optional message.
Display a dynamic "Estimated Price Range" that updates as the user selects options.
This creates engagement and anchors price expectations before the sales call.
Include a micro-copy reassurance line: "No obligation. We respond within 24 hours."

---

### 8. FAQ Section — 300 words

**Layout:** Light background, two-column accordion layout on desktop, single column on mobile.

**Content Strategy:**
Ten questions targeting the most common search queries and sales objections:

1. How long does it take to build a prefab home?
2. Are modular homes as durable as traditional construction?
3. Can I customize the floor plan?
4. What permits do I need in Croatia?
5. What is included in the base price?
6. Are prefab homes energy efficient?
7. Can prefab homes be built on any terrain?
8. What warranty do you offer?
9. How does financing work?
10. What is the difference between modular and prefab?

Each answer should be 25–35 words — enough to satisfy the question and signal expertise,
short enough to maintain engagement. Use schema-ready markup for FAQ rich snippets in Google Search.

## 4. High-Converting Component Layouts

### A. The Niche-Specific Hero Section

**Layout Structure:**

```
[Full-viewport background image with dark overlay]
  ├── Top-left: Logo + Nav (transparent)
  ├── Center: Badge label ("Croatia's #1 Modular Builder")
  ├── Center: H1 — "Your Dream Home. Built in 10 Weeks."
  ├── Center: Subheadline — 20-word value prop
  ├── Center: [Primary CTA Button] + [Secondary Ghost Button]
  └── Bottom strip: 5 trust micro-stats (icon + number + label)
```

**CTA Copy Options:**
- Primary: "Get My Free Estimate" (high intent)
- Secondary: "See Completed Projects" (consideration stage)

**Overlay:** Use `rgba(27,42,59,0.65)` — dark enough for text contrast, light enough to show the home.

**Trust Badges:** Place ISO 9001, Energy Class A+, and "X Years Experience" as inline SVG icons
in the bottom trust strip. These should be visible without scrolling on all viewport sizes.

**Slider Transition:** Use a slow Ken Burns zoom effect (8s) between slides —
creates a premium, architectural feel without being distracting.

### B. Service Cards Grid

**Grid Specs:**

```
Desktop:  2 columns × 2 rows | gap: 24px | max-width: 1200px
Tablet:   2 columns × 2 rows | gap: 16px
Mobile:   1 column            | gap: 16px
```

**Card Anatomy:**

```
[Image — 16:9 aspect ratio, object-fit: cover]
[Accent color top border — 3px]
[Icon — 32px, accent color]
[Headline — 18px, font-weight 700]
[Body — 14px, 3 lines max]
[CTA Link — "Learn More →" in accent color]
```

**Hover Effects:**
- Card lifts with `transform: translateY(-6px)`
- Box shadow deepens: `0 12px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.15)`
- Image scales subtly: `transform: scale(1.04)` with `overflow: hidden`
- Transition: `all 0.3s cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94)`

**Shadow Default:** `0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` — soft, architectural, not cartoonish.

### C. Trust Signals & Interactive Elements

**Trust Badge Types:**

| Badge Type | Placement | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Years in Business | Hero strip, About section | Icon + Bold Number + Label |
| Homes Completed | Hero strip, Why Choose Us | Animated counter on scroll |
| Certifications | Footer, About section | SVG logo lockup |
| Customer Rating | Hero strip, FAQ bottom | Star icons + score + review count |
| Response Time | Estimate form | Inline micro-copy |

**FAQ Accordion Behavior:**
- Default: all closed
- One open at a time (exclusive accordion)
- Smooth `max-height` transition: `0.35s ease`
- Open indicator: rotated chevron (`transform: rotate(180deg)`)
- Active item: left border in accent color (`border-left: 3px solid #4A90A4`)

**Estimate Form Conversion Details:**
- Progress bar at top of form (Step 1 of 3)
- Price range display updates in real-time using JavaScript range mapping
- "Most Popular" badge on the Standard package option
- Form validation with inline error messages (no page reload)
- Submit button copy: "Show Me My Estimate" (not generic "Submit")
- Post-submit: inline thank-you message with expected callback timeframe

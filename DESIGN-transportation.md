# DESIGN BRIEF — Transportation (Limo / Shuttle / Moving / Courier)

> **Wired in code:** `themePreset("transportation")` -> navy #1e3a8a / golden yellow #eab308 | Sora + Inter | soft corners, gradient CTAs, solid nav.
> Read **DESIGN-SYSTEM.md** first — it carries the global rules (type, nav variants,
> buttons, motion, imagery, anti-slop checklist). This brief adds the niche layer.

## Positioning & feel
Punctual, polished, road-certain. Navy dependability with a golden meter running.

## Palette & accent discipline
Preset palette: navy #1e3a8a / golden yellow #eab308. Golden yellow for CTAs, route lines, and number chips; navy is the uniform.

## Typography
Preset pairing: **Sora + Inter** (weights wired automatically).

## Navigation & hero
Static hero — vehicle three-quarter angle, motion implied by light, booking CTA. Solid nav.
- Primary CTA: **"Get a Quote"** | Secondary: **"Book a Ride"**
- Form strategy: **standard — embedded hero/contact forms plus popup CTAs on inner pages**

## Section rhythm
Follow the standard service/location templates (hero -> quick answer -> stats ->
sub-services -> signs -> pros/cons -> comparison -> why-us -> process -> gallery ->
area -> FAQ -> CTA) with the cadence rules from DESIGN-SYSTEM.md s6. Emphasize the
signature sections below; cut anything that would be filler for this niche.

## Signature sections (build these well)
1. Fleet cards — vehicle photo, capacity/luggage chips, from-price
2. Coverage map section with location pills
3. Booking steps — quote, confirm, track, arrive (ProcessSection)
4. Corporate accounts panel — invoicing, priority, dedicated line

## Imagery direction
Clean vehicles at 3/4 angle, chauffeur detail (door, gloves), road landscapes at dawn, careful loading for movers.

**Unsplash starting queries** (verify every result actually shows the subject):
- "black sedan three quarter view"
- "chauffeur opening car door"
- "highway dawn long exposure"
- "moving boxes careful loading"
- "luxury van interior leather"
- "city street taxi motion"

**Never:** stretch-limo prom kitsch, traffic-jam misery, map-pin clipart.

## Copy tone
Minute-accurate. Times, capacities, coverage — promise only what dispatch can keep.

## Do
- Stats: on-time rate, rides completed, years on the road
- Route/coverage visual motif (thin golden lines) used sparingly
- Per-vehicle pages if the fleet is the inventory

## Don't
- Speedometer/speed cliches
- Yellow large-area backgrounds (taxi-sign effect)
- Vague service areas — name the towns

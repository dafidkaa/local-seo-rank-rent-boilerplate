## Positioning & feel
Forensic calm. The niche sells relief from a creeping worry — show dryness, light, and method.

## Palette & accent discipline
Preset palette: navy #1e3a5f / amber #f59e0b. Amber for CTAs and inspection highlights; navy for the engineering trust.

## Typography
Preset pairing: **Poppins + Inter** (weights wired automatically).

## Navigation & hero
Static hero + embedded inspection form — a dry, bright basement after-photo wins. Solid nav.
- Primary CTA: **"Book a Free Inspection"** | Secondary: **"See How It Works"**
- Form strategy: **standard — embedded hero/contact forms plus popup CTAs on inner pages**

## Section rhythm
Follow the standard service/location templates (hero -> quick answer -> stats ->
sub-services -> signs -> pros/cons -> comparison -> why-us -> process -> gallery ->
area -> FAQ -> CTA) with the cadence rules from DESIGN-SYSTEM.md s6. Emphasize the
signature sections below; cut anything that would be filler for this niche.

## Signature sections (build these well)
1. Problem-signs gallery — damp patches, efflorescence (educational, not gross-out)
2. Methods ComparisonTable — interior sealing vs exterior membrane vs drainage
3. Before/after slider — the niche's strongest proof (service.beforeAfter)
4. Warranty panel with plain-language terms and years

## Imagery direction
Basement transformations, membrane application, drainage installs, moisture meters in use, dry bright finished spaces.

**Unsplash starting queries** (verify every result actually shows the subject):
- "basement renovation bright dry"

**Never:** horror-movie mold closeups, flooded disaster porn, generic rain stock.

## Copy tone
Diagnostic and exact: causes, methods, guarantees. Calm the worry with method.

## Do
- Cost-of-inaction section earns its keep here — keep it factual
- Use the inspection process steps to de-risk the first call
- Real warranty years near every CTA

## Don't
- Fear-flooding imagery
- Promising permanent fixes without survey
- Skipping the method comparison — it is the differentiation

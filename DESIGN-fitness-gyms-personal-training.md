# DESIGN BRIEF — Fitness (Gyms / Personal Training / Studios)

> **Wired in code:** `themePreset("fitness")` -> near-black #18181b / lime #84cc16 | Outfit + Inter | sharp corners, gradient CTAs, overlay nav.
> Read **DESIGN-SYSTEM.md** first — it carries the global rules (type, nav variants,
> buttons, motion, imagery, anti-slop checklist). This brief adds the niche layer.

## Positioning & feel
Voltage. High contrast, hard shadows, lime detonations on near-black. The only niche where loud is premium.

## Palette & accent discipline
Preset palette: near-black #18181b / lime #84cc16. Lime for CTAs, stat numerals, and active states ONLY — on black it glows; more than 10 percent coverage and it cheapens.

## Typography
Preset pairing: **Outfit + Inter** (weights wired automatically).
Optional premium upgrade: **Bold Statement — Bebas Neue + Source Sans 3** — set `fontDisplay`/`fontBody` AND
`fontsHref: "https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Bebas+Neue&family=Source+Sans+3:wght@400;500;600;700;800&display=swap"` (this family set needs an explicit URL).

## Navigation & hero
Static hero, overlay nav — one gritty training moment, huge condensed H1, single Start CTA. Dark by default.
- Primary CTA: **"Start Your Trial"** | Secondary: **"Book a Session"**
- Form strategy: **standard — embedded hero/contact forms plus popup CTAs on inner pages**

## Section rhythm
Follow the standard service/location templates (hero -> quick answer -> stats ->
sub-services -> signs -> pros/cons -> comparison -> why-us -> process -> gallery ->
area -> FAQ -> CTA) with the cadence rules from DESIGN-SYSTEM.md s6. Emphasize the
signature sections below; cut anything that would be filler for this niche.

## Signature sections (build these well)
1. Program cards — name, duration, intensity chips, coach avatar
2. Schedule table (real timetable) — crawlable HTML table, mobile-scroll
3. Transformation before/after slider with consented photos only
4. Coach profiles — certifications real, personality forward

## Imagery direction
Chalk dust, barbell knurling macro, dramatic gym light, mid-rep effort, dark backgrounds.

**Unsplash starting queries** (verify every result actually shows the subject):
- "barbell gym dark dramatic"
- "athlete training chalk dust"
- "kettlebell closeup gym floor"
- "personal trainer coaching session"
- "gym interior moody light"
- "running stairs outdoor dawn"

**Never:** mirror selfies, stocky high-fives, neon-sign cliches.

## Copy tone
Imperative, rhythmic, short. Coach voice — demanding but on your side.

## Do
- This niche supports 2-3 dark sections; invert the usual rhythm
- Count-up stats hit hard here: members, sessions, kgs lifted
- Sharp radius everywhere; nothing soft

## Don't
- Lime backgrounds or large lime areas
- Body-shaming copy angles — energy, not guilt
- Slider heroes; one decisive image wins

# Niche Design Brief: Automotive Repair & Detailing
This document defines the visual guidelines, typography, color palette, and asset map for the **Automotive Repair, Maintenance, and Detailing** niche.

## 1. Brand Identity & Visual Strategy
- **Tone & Mood:** The automotive repair and detailing niche demands a brand identity that exudes **trustworthiness, professionalism, and expertise**. Customers in this sector are often driven by a need for reliability and safety, seeking assurance that their vehicles are in capable hands. The emotional drivers include **anxiety over vehicle breakdowns, the desire for a well-maintained and aesthetically pleasing vehicle, and the need for transparent and honest service**. Trust signals are paramount, including certifications, positive reviews, clear communication, and a clean, organized environment. The visual strategy should convey a sense of precision, efficiency, and modern competence. For detailing, there\'s an added layer of **pride in ownership and the desire for a pristine vehicle**, implying a need for visuals that evoke cleanliness, shine, and meticulous care. The overall mood should be reassuring and authoritative, yet approachable.
- **Color Palette Presets:**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Luxury Detailing** | `#1A2B3C` (Deep Navy) | `#2E3E4E` (Dark Grey) | `#FFD700` (Gold) | `#DAA520` (Goldenrod) | `rgba(26, 43, 60, 0.75)` |

  - **Heading Font:** **Oswald** (Google Fonts) - Weights: 700 (Bold), 600 (Semi-Bold). Letter-spacing: -0.02em. (Modern, strong, and legible, conveying professionalism and strength.)

## 4. High-Converting Component Layouts

This section details the CSS/Tailwind design specifications and layout rules for critical high-converting components, ensuring a modern, professional, and user-friendly interface.

### A. The Niche-Specific Hero Section

**Layout & Structure:**
-   **Full-width, responsive hero banner:** `w-full h-[60vh] md:h-[70vh] lg:h-[80vh] relative overflow-hidden`
-   **Background Image:** `bg-cover bg-center` with a subtle parallax effect on scroll (if feasible, otherwise `bg-fixed`). Image should be high-resolution, relevant to the niche (e.g., a modern auto shop exterior or a car being detailed). `z-0`
-   **Overlay:** A semi-transparent dark overlay to ensure text readability. `absolute inset-0 bg-gradient-to-t from-gray-900/70 to-gray-800/50 z-10` (using the `Hero Overlay` hex from the color palette).
-   **Content Container:** Centered vertically and horizontally, constrained width for optimal readability. `relative z-20 flex flex-col items-center justify-center text-center h-full max-w-4xl mx-auto px-4`
-   **Headline (H1):** Large, bold, and impactful. `text-4xl md:text-5xl lg:text-6xl font-extrabold text-white leading-tight mb-4`
-   **Sub-headline (P):** Clear and concise, supporting the main message. `text-lg md:text-xl text-gray-200 mb-8 max-w-2xl`
-   **Call-to-Action (CTA) Buttons:** Prominent, contrasting, and easily clickable. `flex flex-col sm:flex-row gap-4`
    -   **Primary CTA:** `bg-red-600 hover:bg-red-700 text-white font-bold py-3 px-8 rounded-full shadow-lg transition duration-300 ease-in-out transform hover:scale-105`
    -   **Secondary CTA:** `bg-transparent border-2 border-white hover:bg-white hover:text-gray-900 text-white font-bold py-3 px-8 rounded-full transition duration-300 ease-in-out transform hover:scale-105`

**Design Specs:**
-   **Typography:** Headings use `Oswald`, body text uses `Roboto` as defined in Brand Identity.
-   **Color:** Primary CTA uses `Accent (Action Color)` hex. Secondary CTA uses `white` border and text, with `white` background on hover.
-   **Spacing:** Generous vertical spacing to create visual breathing room. `py-20 md:py-24 lg:py-32` for the hero section padding.

### B. Service/Product Grid Cards

**Layout & Structure:**
-   **Grid Container:** Responsive grid adapting to screen size. `grid grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3 gap-8 md:gap-10`
-   **Card Structure:** Each card should be a distinct, clickable unit. `bg-white rounded-lg shadow-md overflow-hidden transition-all duration-300 ease-in-out`
-   **Image:** Top of the card, aspect ratio optimized for visual appeal. `w-full aspect-video object-cover` (or `aspect-square` for detailing services). Placeholder for `service-image-key`.
-   **Content Area:** Padding for text, clear hierarchy. `p-6`
-   **Service Title (H3):** Prominent and descriptive. `text-xl font-semibold text-gray-800 mb-2`
-   **Short Description (P):** Concise summary of the service. `text-gray-600 text-base leading-relaxed mb-4`
-   **Call-to-Action (Link/Button):** "Learn More" or "Get Quote." `inline-flex items-center text-red-600 hover:text-red-800 font-medium`

**Design Specs:**
-   **Hover Effects:** Subtle lift and shadow increase. `hover:shadow-xl transform hover:-translate-y-1`
-   **Shadow Depth:** Initial shadow `shadow-md`, on hover `shadow-xl`.
-   **Aspect Ratios:** `aspect-video` (16:9) for general repair, `aspect-square` (1:1) for detailing services to emphasize precision.
-   **Typography:** Service titles use `Oswald`, descriptions use `Roboto`.
-   **Color:** Text colors from the palette (e.g., `text-gray-800`, `text-gray-600`). CTA link uses `Accent (Action Color)`.

### C. Trust Signals & Interactive Elements

**1. Custom Form Fields (Estimate Form):**
-   **Input Fields:** Clean, modern design with clear labels and focus states. `w-full px-4 py-3 border border-gray-300 rounded-md focus:outline-none focus:ring-2 focus:ring-red-500 focus:border-transparent transition duration-200`
-   **Labels:** `block text-gray-700 text-sm font-medium mb-2`
-   **Textarea:** `min-h-[100px] resize-y`
-   **Submit Button:** Prominent, consistent with primary CTA style. `bg-red-600 hover:bg-red-700 text-white font-bold py-3 px-8 rounded-md shadow-lg transition duration-300 ease-in-out`
-   **Error States:** Clear visual feedback for validation errors. `border-red-500 focus:ring-red-500`

**2. Badges/Certifications:**
-   **Layout:** Displayed in a prominent, easily scannable area (e.g., Trust Bar, footer, or dedicated section). `flex flex-wrap justify-center items-center gap-4`
-   **Badge Style:** Simple, clean, and recognizable. `h-16 w-auto object-contain` (for image-based badges) or `inline-flex items-center px-3 py-1 rounded-full text-sm font-medium bg-green-100 text-green-800` (for text-based certifications).
-   **Hover Effect:** Optional tooltip showing full certification name. `group relative flex justify-center` with `span` for tooltip.

**3. Testimonial Carousel/Slider:**
-   **Container:** Full-width section with subtle background. `bg-gray-50 py-16`
-   **Testimonial Card:** Individual testimonial display. `bg-white p-8 rounded-lg shadow-md text-center`
-   **Quote Text:** `italic text-gray-700 text-lg mb-4`
-   **Author/Source:** `font-semibold text-gray-900`
-   **Navigation:** Simple dots or arrows for control. `flex justify-center mt-8 gap-2` for dots. `bg-gray-300 hover:bg-gray-400 rounded-full w-3 h-3` for individual dots.

**4. Interactive Elements (e.g., Accordion for FAQ):**
-   **Accordion Item:** `border-b border-gray-200`
-   **Question (Header):** `flex justify-between items-center w-full py-4 text-left font-semibold text-gray-800 hover:text-red-600 transition duration-200`
-   **Answer (Content):** `pt-2 pb-4 text-gray-600`
-   **Icon:** Chevron icon for open/close state. `transform transition-transform duration-200` (rotate 180deg when open).

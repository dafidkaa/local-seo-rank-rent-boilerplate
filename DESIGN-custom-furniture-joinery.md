# Niche Design Brief: Custom Furniture & Joinery / Bespoke Carpentry

This document defines the visual guidelines, typography, color palette, and asset map for the **Bespoke Wardrobes, Custom Kitchen Cabinets, Tailored Furniture, Custom Shelving, and Commercial Joinery** niche.

Croatian examples: `namještaj po mjeri osijek`, `namještaj po mjeri split`, `namještaj po mjeri zagreb`

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Visual Strategy

### Tone & Mood

The emotional core of bespoke carpentry is **permanence and pride of ownership**. Homeowners investing in custom furniture are not buying a product — they are commissioning a legacy piece. The visual language must communicate craftsmanship, patience, and mastery. Every design decision should whisper "this was made for you, and it will outlast trends."

Trust is built through **material honesty**. Warm wood tones, raw grain textures, and workshop imagery signal authenticity. Avoid sterile white-box aesthetics. The brand should feel like walking into a master craftsman's studio — ordered, purposeful, and rich with tactile detail. Photography should show hands, tools, sawdust, and finished pieces in real homes.

The conversion psychology leans on **aspiration and exclusivity**. Visitors arrive knowing bespoke costs more. The design must justify that premium instantly. Social proof (named testimonials, project photos, years in trade) and process transparency (how it's made, who makes it) are the two most powerful trust accelerators in this niche.

## 3. Section Architecture & Word Count Distribution

To hit the **1,000–1,500 word density target**, organize sections with alternating light/dark layouts:

1. Hero Slider (150 words)
2. Trust Bar (50 words)
3. Welcome / Story Section (250 words)
4. Service Grid (200 words)
5. Why Choose Us (200 words)
6. Our Process (200 words)
7. Interactive Estimate Form (150 words)
8. FAQ Section (300 words)

---

### Section 1 — Hero Slider `(150 words)`

**Layout:** Full-viewport height slider (2–3 slides). Each slide uses a full-bleed background image with the hero overlay color scrim.

**Content strategy:** Slide 1 leads with the primary geo + service headline (e.g., *"Bespoke Fitted Wardrobes in Zagreb — Designed Around Your Space"*). Slide 2 targets a secondary service. Slide 3 shows a testimonial pull-quote over a finished project photo.

**CTA:** Primary button — *"Get a Free Estimate"* (links to form). Secondary ghost button — *"View Our Work"* (links to gallery or service section).

**Trust micro-copy** beneath CTA: `✓ Free home consultation  ✓ 10-year craftsmanship guarantee  ✓ Local makers`

---

### Section 2 — Trust Bar `(50 words)`

**Layout:** Full-width dark band (`Primary` background), single row of 4–5 icon + stat pairs.

**Content strategy:** Quantified trust signals only. Examples:

- `🪵 14 Years in Trade`
- `⭐ 4.9 / 5 — 180+ Reviews`
- `🏠 600+ Projects Completed`
- `📐 Free Design Consultation`
- `🔨 10-Year Guarantee`

---

### Section 3 — Welcome / Story Section `(250 words)`

**Layout:** Two-column split. Left: `about` image with a floating badge overlay (e.g., *"Est. 2010 — Family Workshop"*). Right: heading + 3 short paragraphs + secondary CTA.

**Content strategy:** Open with a direct empathy statement (*"Most furniture is built for everyone. Ours is built for you."*). Paragraph 2 introduces the founder/team story — years of experience, local roots, trade background. Paragraph 3 articulates the philosophy: no flat-pack, no shortcuts, every joint hand-fitted.

**SEO anchor:** Naturally embed primary keyword phrase in H2 and first paragraph. Include city name in at least one sentence.

---

### Section 4 — Service Grid `(200 words)`

**Layout:** 2×2 or 4-column card grid (responsive: 1-col mobile, 2-col tablet, 4-col desktop).

**Content strategy:** Each card = one core service. Card copy = 40–50 words: service name as H3, 2-sentence benefit description, and a *"Learn More"* or *"Get a Quote"* link. Services: Fitted Wardrobes, Kitchen Cabinets, Custom Shelving & Storage, Commercial Joinery.

**Visual:** Each card uses the corresponding `service1–4` image as a card header with hover zoom effect.

---

### Section 5 — Why Choose Us `(200 words)`

**Layout:** Dark background section (`Primary` color). 3-column icon grid or alternating icon-text rows.

**Content strategy:** 4–6 differentiators, each with a custom icon, bold headline (4–6 words), and 25–30 word explanation. Focus on: local craftsmanship, material sourcing transparency, design collaboration process, installation included, post-install support, and warranty.

**Conversion note:** This section should directly address the #1 objection — *"Why not just buy from IKEA?"* Answer it implicitly through copy about longevity, fit, and uniqueness.

---

### Section 6 — Our Process `(200 words)`

**Layout:** Horizontal numbered stepper (desktop) / vertical timeline (mobile). 4–5 steps.

**Content strategy:** Demystify the commissioning process to reduce friction. Steps:

1. **Free Consultation** — We visit your space, take measurements, discuss your vision.
2. **Design & Quote** — Detailed drawings and transparent fixed-price quote within 5 days.
3. **Crafting** — Built in our local workshop using sustainably sourced timber.
4. **Installation** — Our team fits everything — clean, precise, no subcontractors.
5. **Aftercare** — 10-year guarantee and ongoing support.

**Psychology:** Showing the process reduces perceived risk and makes the purchase feel safe and structured.

---

### Section 7 — Interactive Estimate Form `(150 words)`

**Layout:** Full-width section with `process` image as background + overlay. Centered form card with white/light background.

**Content strategy:** Multi-step or single-step form with 4–5 fields maximum:

- Service type (dropdown)
- Room / project size (dropdown or radio)
- Preferred material/finish (optional dropdown)
- Name + Phone or Email
- *"Get My Free Estimate"* submit button

**Micro-copy:** *"No obligation. We'll call you within 24 hours."* Display a privacy note and a small trust badge (e.g., Google rating).

---

### Section 8 — FAQ Section `(300 words)`

**Layout:** Light background. Two-column accordion layout (desktop) / single-column (mobile). 6–8 questions.

**Content strategy:** Target informational search intent and objection handling simultaneously. Recommended questions:

- *"How long does a bespoke wardrobe take to make?"*
- *"What timber and finishes do you use?"*
- *"Do you offer a design service or do I need my own plans?"*
- *"How much does custom furniture cost compared to off-the-shelf?"*
- *"Do you work across [City/Region]?"*
- *"Is installation included in the price?"*
- *"What guarantee do you offer?"*

**SEO note:** FAQ answers should naturally include long-tail keyword variants and location references. Each answer targets 30–45 words for snippet eligibility.

## 4. High-Converting Component Layouts

### A. The Niche-Specific Hero Section

**Structure:**

```
[Full-viewport image — workshop or installed piece]
[Overlay: rgba(44,26,14,0.62)]
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  EYEBROW: "Bespoke Joinery · Zagreb"│
  │  H1: "Furniture Built for Your      │
  │       Space. Made to Last a         │
  │       Lifetime."                    │
  │  SUBHEAD: 1-sentence value prop     │
  │  [CTA PRIMARY] [CTA GHOST]          │
  │  ✓ Free consult  ✓ 10yr guarantee   │
  └─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Overlay:** Use `Primary Hex` at 60–65% opacity. Never pure black — keep the wood warmth visible through the scrim.

**Trust badges:** Render as inline flex row beneath CTAs. Use SVG checkmark icons in `Accent Hex`. Font: `Lato 700`, 13px, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.06em`.

**Slider controls:** Minimal dot indicators bottom-center. Auto-advance 6 seconds. Pause on hover. No arrow buttons on mobile.

**Mobile adaptation:** Reduce H1 to 2 lines max. Stack CTAs vertically. Trust badges wrap to 2 rows.

---

### B. Service Cards Grid

**Grid spec:**

```
Desktop:  4 columns — gap: 24px
Tablet:   2 columns — gap: 20px
Mobile:   1 column  — gap: 16px
```

**Card anatomy:**

```
┌──────────────────────┐
│  [Image 16:10 ratio] │  ← object-fit: cover, overflow hidden
│  ──────────────────  │
│  Service Name (H3)   │  ← Playfair Display 700, 20px
│  Short description   │  ← Lato 400, 15px, 2 lines max
│  [→ Get a Quote]     │  ← Accent Hex text link with arrow
└──────────────────────┘
```

**Hover effect:**

- Image: `scale(1.04)` transform, `transition: 0.4s ease`
- Card: `box-shadow: 0 12px 40px rgba(44,26,14,0.18)`
- Border: `2px solid Accent Hex` appears on hover
- CTA arrow: translates `4px` right

**Card background:** `Secondary Hex` fill. Rounded corners `8px`. No hard drop shadows at rest — only on hover.

### C. Trust Signals & Interactive Elements

**Google Rating Badge:**

```
┌─────────────────────────┐
│  ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  4.9 / 5      │
│  Based on 180 reviews   │
│  [Google logo SVG]      │
└─────────────────────────┘
```

Render as a floating card on the hero (bottom-left, 24px inset) or as a standalone component in the Trust Bar.

**Accordion FAQ dropdowns:**

- Closed state: question text + `+` icon right-aligned in `Accent Hex`
- Open state: `+` rotates to `×`, answer slides down with `max-height` transition `0.3s ease`
- Active question row: left border `4px solid Accent Hex`, background `Secondary Hex`

**Estimate form conversion details:**

- Submit button: full-width on mobile, `Accent Hex` background, `Accent Dark Hex` on hover
- Input focus state: `border-color: Accent Hex`, `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(AccentHex, 0.15)`

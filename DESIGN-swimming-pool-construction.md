## Positioning & feel
Turquoise serenity with construction backbone. Resort photography, contractor credibility.

## Palette & accent discipline
Preset palette: fresh blue #0369a1 / cyan #06b6d4. Cyan belongs to the water photography; use it in UI only for CTAs and depth markers.

## Typography
Preset pairing: **Plus Jakarta Sans + DM Sans** (weights wired automatically).
Optional premium upgrade: **Geometric Modern — Outfit + Work Sans** — set `fontDisplay`/`fontBody` AND
`fontsHref: "https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Outfit:wght@400;500;600;700;800&family=Work+Sans:wght@400;500;600;700;800&display=swap"` (this family set needs an explicit URL).

## Navigation & hero
Slider hero — finished pools at golden hour; water photography is the brand. Solid nav (overlay optional for luxury builders).
- Primary CTA: **"Design Your Pool"** | Secondary: **"Get a Build Quote"**
- Form strategy: **premium — popup inquiry modal from CTAs; embedded form only on the contact page**

## Section rhythm
Follow the standard service/location templates (hero -> quick answer -> stats ->
sub-services -> signs -> pros/cons -> comparison -> why-us -> process -> gallery ->
area -> FAQ -> CTA) with the cadence rules from DESIGN-SYSTEM.md s6. Emphasize the
signature sections below; cut anything that would be filler for this niche.

## Signature sections (build these well)
1. Build timeline: design, excavation, shell, finish, water (with real durations)
2. Portfolio gallery with lightbox — finished pools, lighting at dusk
3. Finishes & materials comparison (tile/pebble/quartz) table
4. Maintenance plans upsell band for the recurring long-tail

## Imagery direction
Water texture macro, infinity edges, pools at dusk with lighting, construction stage shots.

**Unsplash starting queries** (verify every result actually shows the subject):
- "infinity pool sunset luxury"
- "pool water texture ripples"
- "backyard pool lighting dusk"
- "pool construction excavation"
- "pool tile mosaic underwater"
- "pool deck loungers modern"

**Never:** crowded public pools, inflatable-flamingo kitsch, chlorine-blue oversaturation.

## Copy tone
Resort dreams, contractor specifics. Sell the evening swim, spec the rebar.

## Do
- Full-bleed water imagery sections — let one breathe per page
- Before/after for renovations
- Show build stages honestly; trust is poured with the shell

## Don't
- Renders presented as finished work
- Hiding timeline realities
- Tiny gallery images; pools demand width
